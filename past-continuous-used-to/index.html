📹 Past continuous

An action in progress at a specific moment in the past, or the background to another action.

was / were + verb-ing

They were attacking a cyclist.
They weren't running away.
Was he watching them?

🗃️ Used to

A repeated past habit or state that is no longer true now.

used to + infinitive

He used to be a thief.
He didn't use to live here.
Did they use to know her?

Remember: use the past simple for the shorter completed event: “While the thieves were escaping, the police arrived.” Never use used to for one single event.
Cumberbatch saw them

The attack was continuing when Cumberbatch saw them.
